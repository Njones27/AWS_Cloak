# AWS Cloak privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026.

AWS Cloak does not collect, send or sell any data. Everything it does happens inside your own browser.

## What the extension reads

The extension reads the text of AWS console pages, the AWS access portal and the GovCloud and China consoles so it can find account IDs, ARNs and other sensitive values and blur them on your screen. This text is only checked in memory on your device. It is never saved, logged or sent anywhere.

## What the extension stores

Your settings and the items you hide with the picker are saved with Chrome's local extension storage on your own computer. This includes whether blurring is on, strict mode, tab title masking, blur strength and the page elements you chose to hide. Nothing is synced to a server, and uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

## What the extension sends

Nothing. AWS Cloak makes no network requests, uses no analytics and contains no remote code.

## Sharing

No data is shared with anyone, because no data leaves your browser.
